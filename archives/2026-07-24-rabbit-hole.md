## 👾 GitHub Oddities & Gems

**1. 🐝 block/buzz** – *A hive mind communication platform*  
Imagine a Twitter where your feed is collectively curated by a hive of bees. Buzz is exactly that – a decentralized, swarm-based messaging system that feels like a brain in a jar. Creepy? Yes. Fascinating? Also yes. [Repo](https://github.com/block/buzz)

**2. 🌍 koala73/worldmonitor** – *Your personal CIA dashboard*  
AI-powered news aggregation + geopolitical tracking + infrastructure monitoring, all in one real-time interface. It’s like having a Bloomberg terminal for geopolitics, minus the $2k/month. Privacy warning: you might become addicted to watching shipping routes flicker. [Repo](https://github.com/koala73/worldmonitor)

**3. 🏠 ruvnet/RuView** – *See through walls using WiFi*  
Turn your home router into a radar that detects presence, movement, and even vital signs — without any camera. Frighteningly cool for home automation, slightly terrifying for privacy. The “π RuView” name alone deserves a medal. [Repo](https://github.com/ruvnet/RuView)

**4. 🧩 likec4/likec4** – *Architecture diagrams that never go stale*  
Draw your software architecture in code (C4-style), then see live, interactive diagrams that update when your code changes. No more sticky-note diagrams on a whiteboard that someone erased. [Repo](https://github.com/likec4/likec4)

**5. 📁 yorukot/superfile** – *Terminal file manager that’s actually pretty*  
Remember ranger? This is ranger on caffeine, with a modern TUI that’s slick enough to show off to your CLI-skeptic friends. Blazing fast, FUSE support, and even a preview pane. [Repo](https://github.com/yorukot/superfile)

**6. ✍️ Automattic/harper** – *Grammar checker that works offline, in Rust*  
Because Grammarly selling your prose to the highest bidder is *not* the vibe. Harper is privacy-first, runs entirely on your machine, and is fast enough to check your angry emails before you send them. [Repo](https://github.com/Automattic/harper)

## 🌐 Hacker News Hidden Gems

**“Em dashes are fucking amazing”**  
A love letter to the most elegant punctuation mark. You’ll never use a hyphen—or a comma—again after reading this. Perfect for unleashing your inner grammatical poignancy. [Read & weep (with joy)](https://psychotechnology.substack.com/p/em-dashes-are-fucking-amazing)

**“The case for MUDs in modern times”**  
Text-based multiplayer worlds are *alive*, and they might be the perfect antidote to algorithmic feeds. No graphics, no microtransactions – just you, a dungeon, and a dragon that types “You have been slain.” Pure magic. [Take the red pill](https://www.andrewzigler.com/feed/the-case-for-muds-in-modern-times)

**“Game of Trees – Git-based version control from OpenBSD folks”**  
If Git is a Swiss army knife, Game of Trees is a katana. It’s a minimalist, secure reimplementation of Git (mostly compatible) that emphasizes simplicity and correctness. OpenBSD devs know their stuff. [Hey, it’s called Game of Trees](https://www.gameoftrees.org/)

**“Ah, the Codeberg Drama”**  
A short story about disagreements, forks, and the eternal tension between governance and code. Not to be missed if you enjoy open-source soap operas. [Popcorn recommended](https://gagliardoni.net/#20260724_codeberg_drama)

**“The IBM PC, Part 1: Arrival”**  
A deep-dive into the machine that started it all — 45 years ago this month. Perfect for nostalgic greybeards and curious youngsters who wonder why we still call keyboards “IBM-style”. [Part 1 is live](https://technicshistory.com/2026/07/24/the-ibm-pc-part-1-arrival/)
