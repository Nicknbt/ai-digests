## 🧠 Trending GitHub repos worth poking

**1. [`reverse-skill`](https://github.com/zhaoxuya520/reverse-skill)**  
A "skill router" for AI coding agents that want to do reverse engineering / authorized pentesting. It routes your AI to the right toolchain, bootstraps what’s needed on demand, and keeps a self-evolving knowledge base. Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Cline, and friends.  
*Motto: "hold my beer, I’ll disassemble that firmware."*  
(Please only use this on stuff you own.)

**2. [`ESP32-Bit-Pirate`](https://github.com/geo-tp/ESP32-Bit-Pirate)**  
A hardware hacking tool with a web-based CLI that "speaks every protocol." I2C, SPI, UART, maybe even the protocol of the ancient aliens. It’s an ESP32, so the price is right, and the fun is guaranteed.

**3. [`openwork`](https://github.com/different-ai/openwork)**  
Open-source alternative to Claude Cowork, powered by opencode. If you want the "AI teammate in the loop" thing without betting your entire workflow on one vendor, this is your jam.

**4. [`last30days-skill`](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill)**  
An AI skill that researches a topic across Reddit, X, YouTube, HN, Polymarket, and the wider web, then synthesizes a grounded summary. Perfect for when you need the internet’s pulse but don’t want to open 47 tabs.

**5. [`awesome-systematic-trading`](https://github.com/paperswithbacktest/awesome-systematic-trading)**  
Curated libraries, strategies, books, and tutorials for systematic trading. Great way to spend a weekend building a bot that loses money more systematically.

**6. [`microsoft/AI-For-Beginners`](https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners)**  
12 weeks, 24 lessons, AI for everyone. A classic for a reason.

**7. [`tuicr`](https://github.com/agavra/tuicr)**  
A code review TUI with vim keybindings. Finally: you can review pull requests in the same terminal where you pretend to be a sysadmin.

*Also trending: [`chatwoot`](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot) — open-source Intercom/Zendesk alternative, and [`kaneo`](https://github.com/usekaneo/kaneo) — "All you need. Nothing you don’t." Rare claim in project management software.*
